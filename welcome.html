Capture, record and extract anything you can see

Lassolve puts screenshots, screen recording, a design inspector, a data scraper and a media finder in one button. Everything runs on your device.

1

Pin Lassolve

Click the puzzle piece in Chrome’s toolbar, then the pin next to Lassolve, so it’s one click away.

2

Capture or record

Drag an area, draw a lasso, click an element, or capture the whole page. Annotate, blur and download as an image or PDF.

3

Extract what you need

Turn lists and tables into spreadsheets, find every image, video, audio and file, and reveal hidden content.

Shortcuts

Open LassolveAlt + Shift + L
Capture an areaAlt + Shift + A
Capture the full pageAlt + Shift + F
Scrape dataAlt + Shift + E
Everything elseRight-click any page and choose Lassolve

Change shortcuts at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Mark for feedback

In the editor, choose ✓ Mark for feedback to mark a screenshot with red ink, ticks and crosses, marks with a total, comments from a comment bank, and short audio comments. Save the marked image, or a feedback page students can open in any browser to see the marks and hear the audio.

From the address bar

Click Chrome’s address bar, type lasso and a space, then one of these and press Enter. Suggestions appear as you type.

Use it responsibly

Lassolve only saves files that a page already shows openly. It doesn’t bypass logins, paywalls, DRM or streaming protection. Respect copyright, each site’s terms, and people’s privacy, especially when scraping contact details.
