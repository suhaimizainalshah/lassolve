# Lassolve privacy policy

**Last updated:** November 2026

Lassolve works **on your own device**. It does not collect, sell or send the pages you visit, your captures, your recordings, the data you extract, your browsing history or any personal information to anyone. There are no accounts, analytics, trackers or external servers.

## What is stored, and where
- **Captures** you take are kept in your browser’s own storage (IndexedDB), limited to your 30 most recent, so you can edit and download them later. Delete them any time in *My captures*.
- **Recorder settings** (such as quality and microphone on or off) are kept in Chrome’s local extension storage.
- **Recordings** stay in the recorder tab until you download them. They are not stored by Lassolve.
- **GIFs** are made on your device, from your recording or a video file you choose. Neither the video nor the GIF is uploaded.
- **The microphone meter** measures loudness on your device, to show your voice is being picked up. Nothing is recorded until you start a recording.

## Permissions
- **activeTab** and **scripting**: to run Lassolve’s tools on the page you are on, only when you click Lassolve, use a shortcut or the right-click menu.
- **downloads**: to save images, files and data you choose.
- **storage**: to remember your settings.
- **contextMenus**: to add Lassolve to the right-click menu.
- **Screen, microphone and camera**: only when you start a recording, through Chrome’s own permission prompts.

## Your responsibilities
Only download or reuse content you have the right to use, respect each website’s terms, and handle any personal data you extract in line with privacy law.
